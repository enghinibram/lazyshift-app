Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data LazyShift collects when you use app.lazyshift.com and lazyshift.com, why we collect it, who we share it with and what your rights are.

We process personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR"). For users in the United Kingdom, we also process it under the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and the Data Protection Act 2018. In this policy, "GDPR" refers to both where they apply.

1. Who we are

LazyShift is operated by Ibram Enghin, an individual (not a company) based in Romania, who is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: dev.lazylabs@gmail.com. Postal address available on request by email to dev.lazylabs@gmail.com.

3. Cookies and local storage

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics, _ga and _ga_*) are set only if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until then Google Analytics is not loaded at all. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page; if you withdraw consent we delete the analytics cookies we can reach. Your choice is stored separately on lazyshift.com and app.lazyshift.com. Apart from these analytics cookies, LazyShift's code does not set any cookies.

The app also keeps some data in your browser's local storage, on your device. It does this so you can use the app offline, and to remember your theme, your cookie choice and the free features you have used. We do not use session storage. These items are strictly necessary for the service or something you asked for, so they do not need consent:

KeyWhat it holds and whyWhen it is removed

The app's service worker also keeps an offline cache (named lazyshift-v followed by a version number) with copies of the app's own pages, scripts, styles, icons and the Supabase library, so the app opens without a network connection. It contains no personal data. Each new version of the app replaces it, and it is removed when you clear site data.

Unlike cookies, local storage and the offline cache are not sent automatically with requests to any server; the app reads them in your browser. The one exception is your sign-in session, which the Supabase library uses to authenticate your requests to Supabase. Data you save to your account, such as your schedule, and the PDF export email are sent to us separately, as described in section 2.

To clear this data, sign out (this needs an internet connection) to remove your session, profile and schedule copy, or clear the site data for app.lazyshift.com in your browser's settings to remove everything listed here, including the offline cache.

We do not use advertising cookies or any advertising or cross-site tracking technology.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers we need to run LazyShift:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Your data is stored in Supabase's West EU (Ireland) region. (Supabase privacy policy)
  • Cloudflare — DNS and proxy for our domains; it processes your IP address and request data to deliver and protect the site. (Cloudflare privacy policy)
  • GitHub Pages — static hosting of the website and app. (GitHub privacy statement)
  • Vercel — hosts the serverless function that will receive subscription notifications from Lemon Squeezy once Pro is on sale. The app itself does not call it. (Vercel privacy policy)
  • Lemon Squeezy — payments, once Pro is launched. Lemon Squeezy will act as our reseller and Merchant of Record: it collects your payment and billing details directly and processes them under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC) — website analytics, only after you consent. (Google privacy policy)
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla) — delivers shift reminders if you turn them on.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it or to protect our legal rights.

5. International transfers

Our database is in the European Union (Ireland). The UK recognises the EU as providing adequate protection, so data of UK users can be held there. Some other providers (including Cloudflare, GitHub, Vercel, Google and Lemon Squeezy) may process data in the United States or other countries outside the EU and the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, where the provider is certified) or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for UK users' data. You can ask us for more information.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and schedule data — until your account is deleted, then removed within 30 days.
  • Push subscriptions and reminder records — until you turn off notifications for LazyShift in your browser, the browser withdraws the subscription, or your account is deleted.
  • Billing records — 6 years.
  • PDF export email — until you ask us to delete it.
  • Support emails — 2 years.
  • Google Analytics data — for the retention period set in Google Analytics, at most 14 months.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and, for UK users, UK GDPR) you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to our processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this does not affect processing that happened before).

To delete your account and data, or to exercise any other right, email dev.lazylabs@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We reply within one month.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority:

  • the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP): www.dataprotection.ro;
  • if you are in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

If you live in another EU country, you can also complain to the supervisory authority there.

9. Children

LazyShift is intended for working adults and is not directed at children under 16.

10. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, access to our database is limited to the services that need it, and passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; we will tell you about significant changes before they take effect.
