3. Cookies and local storage

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics, _ga and _ga_*) are set only if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until then Google Analytics is not loaded at all. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page; if you withdraw consent we delete the analytics cookies we can reach. Your choice is stored separately on lazyshift.com and app.lazyshift.com.

We also use your browser's local storage for things that are strictly necessary for the service or that you asked for, which do not require consent: your sign-in session, your cookie choice, your light/dark theme, the email you entered for PDF exports, and whether you have used your free salary calculation.

We do not use advertising cookies or any advertising or cross-site tracking technology.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to our processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this does not affect processing that happened before).

To delete your account and data, or to exercise any other right, email contact@lazyshift.com from the email address linked to your account. We reply within one month. Deleting your account does not cancel a Lemon Squeezy subscription — please cancel it first (see our Refund & Cancellation Policy).

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

9. Children

LazyShift is intended for working adults and is not directed at children under 16.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Database access is restricted with row-level security so that each account can only read its own data, and passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth.
