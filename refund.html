1. Refunds and cancellation

Payments are non-refundable, except where the law gives you a right to a refund. You can try LazyShift free for 30 days without entering a card. You can cancel your subscription at any time; you keep Pro access until the end of the period you have paid for. If you are a consumer in the UK, you have a statutory right to cancel within 14 days of subscribing. By subscribing you ask us to give you immediate access. If you then cancel within the 14 days, you pay for the time you have used and we refund the unused part. We reply to refund requests within 5 working days.

2. Who handles your payment

Pro subscriptions will be sold through Lemon Squeezy, which will act as our reseller and Merchant of Record: it takes your payment, charges and accounts for VAT, issues your receipts and invoices, and pays approved refunds back to your original payment method. Lemon Squeezy's buyer terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.

3. How to cancel

Use the subscription management link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or email dev.lazylabs@gmail.com from your account email and we will cancel it for you. After cancelling you will not be charged again, and your account continues with the free features once your paid period ends.

4. How to request a refund

Email dev.lazylabs@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout and your order number from the Lemon Squeezy receipt.
