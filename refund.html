Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Who handles your payment

LazyShift Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Lemon Squeezy takes your payment, charges and accounts for VAT, and issues your receipts and invoices. Refunds are paid back by Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method. Lemon Squeezy's buyer terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.

2. Free trial

The 30-day free trial requires no payment card and nothing is charged when it ends, so there is nothing to cancel or refund for the trial.
